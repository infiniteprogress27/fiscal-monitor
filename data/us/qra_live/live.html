全文红线

WASHINGTON — The U.S. Department of the Treasury is offering $125 billion of Treasury securities to refund approximately $96.3 $97 billion of privately-held Treasury notes and bonds maturing on August November 15, 2026. This issuance will raise new cash from private investors of approximately $28.7 $28 billion. The securities are:

A 3-year note in the amount of $58 billion, maturing August November 15, 2029;

A 10-year note in the amount of $42 billion, maturing August November 15, 2036; and

A 30-year bond in the amount of $25 billion, maturing August November 15, 2056.

The 3-year note will be auctioned at 1:00 p.m. EDT ET on Tuesday, August 11, Monday, November 9, 2026. The 10-year note will be auctioned at 1:00 p.m. EDT ET on Wednesday, August 12, Tuesday, November 10, 2026. The 30-year bond will be auctioned at 1:00 p.m. EDT ET on Thursday, August 13, November 12, 2026. All these auctions will take place on a yield basis and will settle on Monday, August 17, November 16, 2026.

The balance of Treasury financing requirements over the quarter will be met with regular weekly bill auctions, cash management bills (CMBs), and monthly note, bond, Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), and 2-year Floating Rate Note (FRN) auctions.

NOMINAL COUPON AND FRN FINANCING

Treasury believes its current auction sizes leave it well positioned to address potential changes to the fiscal outlook and to the size and composition of the SOMA portfolio. Based on current projected borrowing needs, Treasury anticipates maintaining nominal coupon and FRN auction sizes for at least the next several quarters. Treasury is monitoring SOMA purchases of Treasury bills and growing demand for Treasury bills from the private sector. Looking ahead, Treasury continues to evaluate potential future changes to nominal coupon and FRN auction sizes, with a focus on trends in structural demand and potential costs and risks of various issuance profiles. As part of that process, Treasury will engage with a variety of market participants to better understand evolving Treasury supply and demand dynamics. In considering any changes, Treasury will be guided by the objective of financing the government at the lowest cost over time.

As always, Treasury will continue to evaluate near-term borrowing needs and assess additional adjustments to bill auction sizes as appropriate.

CONVERSION OF 7-YEAR NOTE TO QUARTERLY NEW ISSUE

Treasury plans to convert the 7-year note from a monthly new issue to a quarterly new issue with two reopenings (similar to the 10-year note). The 7-year note will continue to be auctioned towards the end of each month and issue and mature at month end. Treasury will auction new issues in March, June, September, and December and conduct reopening auctions in the subsequent two months of each cycle. This conversion will go into effect beginning with the March 2027 new issue, which will be reopened in April and May.
