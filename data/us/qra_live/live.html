CONVERSION OF 7-YEAR NOTE TO QUARTERLY NEW ISSUE
  • Treasury plans to convert the 7-year note from a monthly new issue to a quarterly new issue with two reopenings (similar to the 10-year note).

  • This conversion will go into effect beginning with the March 2027 new issue, which will be reopened in April and May.
